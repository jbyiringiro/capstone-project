# Vuga design illustrations

These 25 original SVG illustrations were created for the local Vuga design concept
in `design/`. They are visual design candidates, not a validated AAC
symbol system. Kinyarwanda labels and symbol recognition require local review.

The illustrated-symbol preference can be turned off to use the existing pictogram
set. Symbols without a matching new illustration continue to use their existing
image. ARASAAC assets remain in `/symbols/` with their separate attribution and
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms; these original illustrations do not change those terms.
