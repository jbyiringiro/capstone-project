Abafashe amafoto n’uburenganzira bwo kuyakoresha

Aya mafoto yaturutse kuri interineti. Buri foto ifite uwafashe ifoto, aho yaturutse n’uruhushya rwo kuyikoresha. Yagabanyijwe mu bunini, ishyirwa ku buso bwa kare kandi ibikwa mu buryo bwa WebP; nta gice cyayo cyaciwe.

Amafoto arimo abantu ntiyemeza ko abo bantu bafite ubumuga cyangwa ko bakoresha Vuga. Amashusho n’amagambo biracyakeneye gusuzumwa n’abazabikoresha.
