Warm colour, clear meaning

Dark labels on quiet surfaces. Colour supports the symbol and its label.

Teal is the primary action colour. Pastels frame content; they do not encode a diagnosis or a universal AAC colour system. Retain a learner’s familiar symbols and category colours when appropriate.

What to validate at the school

Meaning

Do these symbols and words represent what the learner wants to say, including refusal, help, feelings and social connection?

Access

Can the learner find, see and select the desired item comfortably? Does the number of visible choices suit them?

Preference

Ask about colour, illustration style and motion. Use the learner’s preferences; an attractive interface is not the same as an effective communication aid.
